Reported Speech: dari Langsung jadi Tidak Langsung

Setiap hari kamu menyampaikan ulang apa yang orang lain bilang — "Manager bilang deadline-nya mundur", "QA bilang bug-nya udah kefix". Itu semua namanya reported speech (indirect speech). Materi ini membongkar cara mengubah kutipan langsung ("...") jadi kalimat laporan yang natural, plus aturan mundur tense yang jadi jantung dari topik ini — namanya backshift.

01 Tujuan pembelajaran

Apa yang akan kamu kuasai

Setelah materi ini selesai, kamu akan bisa:

// backshift

Menggeser tense dengan benar

Mengubah simple present, present continuous, present perfect, simple past, dan modal verb jadi bentuk "mundur satu langkah" saat dilaporkan.

// pronoun

Mengganti kata ganti & possessive

I/my berubah jadi he/his atau she/her sesuai siapa yang melapor dan siapa yang dilaporkan.

// waktu & tempat

Mengubah penanda waktu & tempat

now→then, today→that day, here→there, dan penanda relatif lainnya.

// pertanyaan & perintah

Melaporkan pertanyaan & perintah

Mengubah "Are you...?" jadi asked if..., dan "Close the door." jadi told...to close...

02 Konsep inti

Direct speech, indirect speech, dan backshift

Ada dua cara menyampaikan ucapan orang lain: mengutip persis (direct), atau menceritakan ulang dengan kata-katamu sendiri (indirect / reported). Keduanya sering ketuker, jadi kita samakan dulu istilahnya.

Direct speech mengutip persis, pakai tanda kutip: Rina said, "I am busy." Di sini kamu mengulang kata-kata Rina apa adanya.

Indirect speech (reported speech) menceritakan ulang tanpa tanda kutip, dan biasanya digabung dengan kata sambung that (boleh dihilangkan secara lisan): Rina said (that) she was busy. Perhatikan tiga hal yang berubah: tanda kutip hilang, I jadi she, dan am jadi was.

◆ Kenapa tense-nya mundur?

Karena saat kamu melaporkan ucapan seseorang, itu terjadi setelah ucapan aslinya diucapkan — bahkan cuma beda beberapa detik. Bahasa Inggris "menandai" jarak waktu ini dengan memundurkan tense satu langkah. Ini yang disebut backshift. Kalau reporting verb-nya (said, told, asked) berbentuk past tense, hampir semua tense di dalam kutipan ikut mundur satu langkah.

◆
Yang tidak mundur lagi: kalau tense aslinya sudah past perfect, atau modal-nya sudah would/could/might/should/ought to/used to — bentuk itu sudah paling mundur, tidak berubah lagi. Contoh: "I would help if I could" tetap "he said he would help if he could".
03 Kenapa penting

Kenapa materi ini penting

Karena hampir tidak mungkin kerja tim tanpa menyampaikan ulang apa yang orang lain bilang. Bug report, notulen meeting, forward email klien, catatan standup — semuanya reported speech:

  • Tiket bug: "The user said the page had frozen after clicking submit."
  • Notulen meeting: "The PM said the deadline had moved to next week."
  • Forward dari klien: "The client asked if the feature was ready."
◆ Kenapa presisi itu penting di sini

Salah melaporkan bisa bikin miskomunikasi serius. Bandingkan: "He said the API was ready" vs "He said the API wasn't ready" — cuma beda satu kata "not", tapi dampaknya ke keputusan tim bisa sangat besar. Reported speech yang akurat adalah skill komunikasi profesional, bukan cuma latihan grammar.

04 Contoh

Contoh sederhana

Tujuh pasangan direct → indirect paling dasar — perhatikan pola tense, pronoun, dan kata tunjuknya.

  • "I am hungry," she said. → She said she was hungry.
  • "I work remotely," he said. → He said he worked remotely.
  • "I have finished," she said. → She said she had finished.
  • "I will call you," he said. → He said he would call me.
  • "I can help," she said. → She said she could help.
  • "This is my laptop," he said. → He said that was his laptop.
  • "I am here," she said. → She said she was there.
05 Konteks nyata

Contoh dalam kehidupan sehari-hari

Bayangkan situasi harian yang biasa kamu alami:

  • Ibu di telepon: "I am cooking dinner." → My mother said she was cooking dinner. (Ibu bilang dia sedang masak makan malam.)
  • Chat temen: "I'll be there in 10 minutes." → He said he would be there in ten minutes. (Dia bilang dia akan sampai 10 menit lagi.)
  • Tetangga cerita: "We are moving next month." → They said they were moving the following month. (Mereka bilang akan pindah bulan depan.)
  • Pasangan bilang: "I miss you." → She said she missed me. (Dia bilang dia kangen.)
◆ Perhatikan

next month berubah jadi the following month karena itu penanda waktu relatif — relatif terhadap kapan kalimat aslinya diucapkan, bukan terhadap kapan kamu melaporkannya sekarang.

06 Konteks nyata

Contoh dalam dunia kerja

  • Manager di meeting: "The deadline is Friday." → The manager said the deadline was Friday.
  • HR lewat email: "You need to submit the form by tomorrow." → HR told me I needed to submit the form the next day.
  • Klien lewat email: "We are not satisfied with the current UI." → The client said they were not satisfied with the current UI.
  • Rekan kerja: "I sent the file yesterday." → She said she had sent the file the day before.

Meneruskan pesan secara akurat — tanpa menambah atau menghilangkan makna — adalah salah satu skill komunikasi paling dibutuhkan di lingkungan kerja profesional, terutama saat kamu jadi "jembatan" antara klien, manager, dan tim.

07 Konteks nyata

Contoh dalam software engineering

Reported speech ada di mana-mana dalam dunia kerja teknis — dari bug report sampai catatan standup.

bug-report-notes.mdLOG
// Ucapan asli di Slack thread
QA: "The login button is broken."
Client: "The app crashes when I upload a large file."
PM: "We will push the release tomorrow."

// Dirangkum jadi tiket, pakai reported speech
QA said the login button was broken.
The client said the app crashed when they uploaded a large file.
The PM said they would push the release the next day.
  • Bug report: "The user said the page had frozen after clicking submit."
  • Komentar PR review: "The reviewer mentioned that the function needed a null check."
  • Standup: "Yesterday, I said I was blocked on the payment API."
◆ Di daily standup

"Yesterday, I told the team I was still debugging the payment flow, and today I'm confirming it's fixed." Perhatikan polanya tetap sama: reporting verb (told) + subjek + tense yang sudah backshift (was still debugging).

08 Awas salah

Kesalahan yang sering dilakukan

Empat kesalahan paling umum — kenali polanya supaya tidak terulang.

Kesalahan #1 — Lupa backshift tense. Ini kesalahan paling umum: tense di dalam kutipan dibiarkan sama seperti aslinya.

salah — tense tidak mundur✕ SALAH
She said she is busy.
He said he will call.
benar — tense mundur satu langkah✓ BENAR
She said she was busy.
He said he would call.

Kesalahan #2 — Lupa ganti pronoun/possessive.

salah — pronoun tidak diganti✕ SALAH
He said I am tired.
benar — pronoun ikut sudut pandang pelapor✓ BENAR
He said he was tired.
◆
Kesalahan #3 — Menyimpan urutan kata tanya. ❌ She asked where did I live. Reported question memakai urutan kalimat berita, bukan kalimat tanya — jadi tidak ada do/does/did, tidak ada inversi. Yang benar: ✓ She asked where I lived.
◆ Kesalahan #4 — "say" dipakai seperti "tell"

tell WAJIB diikuti objek orang langsung, say TIDAK. ❌ She said me she was busy. Yang benar: ✓ She told me she was busy. atau ✓ She said (to me) she was busy.

09 Cara mengingat

Tips mengingat materi

  • Akronim "BPQ" — Backshift tense, Pronoun/possessive diganti, Quantifier waktu & tempat berubah. Cek tiga hal ini setiap kali mengubah kalimat.
  • Bayangkan reported speech seperti "menceritakan ulang keesokan harinya" — otomatis waktunya mundur satu langkah, sama seperti kamu cerita ke teman soal kejadian kemarin.
  • Untuk pertanyaan, ingat: "reported question = kalimat berita, bukan kalimat tanya" — tidak ada tanda tanya, tidak ada do/does/did, subjek di depan kata kerja.
  • Untuk perintah/permintaan, ingat pola verb + object + to + infinitive: told/asked someone to do something.

Pesan yang diteruskan harus tetap utuh maknanya, walau bentuk kalimatnya berubah total.

— prinsip dasar komunikasi tim yang efektif

Artinya: fokus utama reported speech bukan menghafal rumus, tapi memastikan makna ucapan asli tetap sampai dengan benar — walau bentuk kata, tense, dan urutannya berubah drastis.

10 Ringkasan

Ringkasan materi

  • Direct speech mengutip persis, indirect/reported speech menceritakan ulang tanpa tanda kutip.
  • Backshift — tense & modal mundur satu langkah kalau reporting verb-nya past tense.
  • Pronoun & possessive berubah sesuai sudut pandang si pelapor.
  • Penanda waktu/tempat relatif berubah: now→then, today→that day, here→there.
  • Reported question pakai urutan kalimat berita + if/whether untuk yes/no. Reported command pakai to + infinitive.

◆ Perhatikan

Semua respons "Kamu" pakai reported speech: said they were, asked if we could, said they needed, mentioned that... had increased. Dalam percakapan langsung seperti ini (masih di hari yang sama), penanda waktu kadang tidak diubah total — tapi tense-nya tetap backshift.

14 Praktik

Writing exercise

Ambil 5 kalimat direct speech dari percakapan nyatamu (chat WA, email kerja, komentar code review, obrolan keluarga), lalu ubah semuanya jadi reported speech. Contoh kerangka:

  • Kalimat dari atasan/klien — "He/She said (that) ..."
  • Pertanyaan yang kamu terima — "He/She asked if/whether ..."
  • Pertanyaan wh-question — "He/She asked where/when/why ..."
  • Perintah atau permintaan — "He/She told/asked me to ..."
  • Sesuatu yang dijanjikan seseorang — "He/She promised (that) ..."
◆ Kirimkan hasilnya

Kirimkan hasil tulisanmu di chat kapan saja — akan dikoreksi backshift tense, pronoun, dan kealamian kalimatnya sebelum kamu lanjut ke Materi 3.

15 Praktik

Speaking exercise

Ucapkan keras-keras (boleh rekam suara sendiri untuk didengar ulang):

  1. "My manager said the deadline had moved to next week."
  2. "I told my teammate I was still fixing the bug."
  3. "She asked if the API was ready."
  4. "He asked me to review the pull request."

Fokus pada kejelasan said/told/asked dan bentuk backshift-nya (had moved, was, to review) — jangan tergesa sampai bentuk mundurnya "hilang" saat diucapkan cepat.

17 Perkaya bacaan

Reading recommendation

"During the sprint review, the product manager explained that the new feature had been tested successfully. She mentioned that the QA team had found only two minor bugs, and she promised that both would be fixed before the release. When asked if the timeline was still on track, she confirmed that it was."

Paragraf pendek ini sengaja dipadatkan dengan reported speech supaya kamu langsung latihan mengenali polanya dalam teks utuh, bukan cuma kalimat lepas.

Kalau sudah lancar, coba baca changelog atau deskripsi pull request di GitHub project besar mana pun — sering ada kalimat seperti "A user reported that the export function was crashing" atau "The maintainer mentioned that this issue had been fixed in v2.3."

18 Perkaya kosakata

Vocabulary list — reporting verbs (18 kata)

Semua kata ini adalah alternatif dari say/tell/ask yang lebih spesifik — makin kamu kuasai, makin natural reported speech-mu terdengar.

say/seɪ/bilang / mengatakan

verb — Contoh: "She said she was tired." Tips: tidak diikuti objek orang langsung; kalau perlu, pakai "say to someone".

tell/tel/memberitahu

verb — Contoh: "He told me the build had failed." Tips: WAJIB diikuti objek orang langsung (tell + someone).

ask/ɑːsk/bertanya / meminta

verb — Contoh: "She asked if the API was ready." Dipakai untuk reported question maupun reported request.

explain/ɪkˈspleɪn/menjelaskan

verb — Contoh: "He explained that the bug was caused by a race condition." Sinonim: clarify.

mention/ˈmenʃn/menyebutkan (sekilas)

verb — Contoh: "She mentioned that the client had approved the design." Tips: lebih ringan/singkat dari "explain".

claim/kleɪm/mengklaim (belum tentu benar)

verb — Contoh: "He claimed that he had fixed the bug." Tips: sering menyiratkan keraguan si pelapor.

admit/ədˈmɪt/mengakui

verb — Contoh: "She admitted that she had forgotten to push the code." Antonim: deny.

deny/dɪˈnaɪ/menyangkal

verb — Contoh: "He denied that he had broken the build." Antonim: admit.

warn/wɔːrn/memperingatkan

verb — Contoh: "The senior warned us not to deploy on Friday." Pola: warn + object + not to + infinitive.

advise/ədˈvaɪz/menyarankan

verb — Contoh: "She advised me to write more tests." Pola sama seperti "warn" (+ object + to infinitive).

insist/ɪnˈsɪst/bersikeras

verb — Contoh: "He insisted that the feature was ready." Menunjukkan keteguhan sikap si pembicara.

suggest/səˈdʒest/menyarankan (opsi)

verb — Contoh: "She suggested that we refactor the module." Tips: sering diikuti pola khusus (we refactor, bukan we refactored).

complain/kəmˈpleɪn/mengeluh / komplain

verb — Contoh: "The client complained that the app was slow."

promise/ˈprɒmɪs/berjanji

verb — Contoh: "He promised he would fix it by Monday." Pola: promise + would + verb.

remind/rɪˈmaɪnd/mengingatkan

verb — Contoh: "She reminded me to update the changelog." Pola: remind + object + to infinitive.

wonder/ˈwʌndər/bertanya-tanya (dalam hati)

verb — Contoh: "He wondered if the server was down." Tips: lebih lembut dari "ask", sering untuk pertanyaan retoris.

confirm/kənˈfɜːrm/mengonfirmasi

verb — Contoh: "She confirmed that the release had been deployed."

announce/əˈnaʊns/mengumumkan

verb — Contoh: "The PM announced that the sprint had been extended."

19 Perkaya ucapan

Pronunciation tips

  • said /sed/ — banyak pemula salah ucap jadi "seyd" (seperti "say" + d). Ingat: said berima dengan "bed", bukan dengan "made".
  • weak "that" — dalam reported speech lisan, that sering diucapkan sangat pendek/lemah, bahkan sering dihilangkan sepenuhnya: "She said (that) she was busy."
  • kontraksi 'd yang ambigu — 'd bisa berarti had ATAU would. Bedakan dari kata kerja setelahnya: "She said she'd finished" (had + V3) vs "She said she'd call" (would + base verb).
  • "asked if/whether" — diucapkan menyambung cepat, seperti "ɑːskt ɪf".
◆ Latihan cepat

Coba ucapkan berulang: "She said she'd already finished, but he said he'd call later." Perhatikan dua 'd yang beda arti tapi bunyinya identik — konteks kata kerja setelahnya yang membedakan.
